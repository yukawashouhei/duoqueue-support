Privacy Policy

Effective September 14, 2026

Your game library

Duo Queue stores game titles, tags, play status, ratings, moods, notes, chosen cover photos, source links, imported friends' lists, game-night nicknames, shortlists, choices, plans and memories on your device. We do not operate an account service, social network or backend for this content. We do not send game content or game-night choices to RevenueCat.

Game discovery and rooms

The FreeToGame catalog and artwork connect to FreeToGame; filtering that catalog happens on your device. When IGDB search is connected, the title you search and requested game IDs are sent to our catalog service hosted by Google Cloud and then to IGDB. IGDB serves the game artwork directly. This service does not receive your private library, notes, personal photos or room choices. The service caches public game responses temporarily; we do not add application request logs. Providers may process IP addresses and ordinary network information under their own infrastructure policies. A working catalog connection is shown in the app; an unconnected service does not offer console search.

A .duoroom file includes its room name, game references, participant nicknames and choices. It includes public catalog metadata and artwork URLs, not the image files, your personal photos or private notes. The file is sent only through a share action you choose. Returned documents merge participant updates on the receiving device; there is no automatic room synchronization.

Sources: IGDB, FreeToGame and Google Cloud Privacy Notice.

Purchases

Apple processes payment, and RevenueCat verifies and restores access to the one-time purchase. RevenueCat receives purchase and receipt or transaction information, an anonymous app user identifier, and device/app information needed for purchase processing and purchase analytics in the RevenueCat dashboard. No name or email is requested by the app for a purchase. We do not use advertising, tracking across other companies' apps or websites, or advertising SDKs.

These services have their own policies: RevenueCat Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy.

Source links and images

When you share or open a game source link, Apple's LinkPresentation may contact the linked website to fetch a title or thumbnail. That website can receive ordinary network information such as your IP address. Source thumbnails are stored as a local cache and are excluded from generated sharing cards and exported lists. If you choose a personal cover photo using Apple's photo picker, the selected photo is stored locally; the app does not require access to your entire photo library.

What you choose to share

A .duoqueue file contains your chosen display name and game titles, normalized titles, tags, status and export date. Source URLs are included only when you enable Include source links in exports. Use a nickname instead of your legal name. Cards can contain selected game titles, ratings, moods and notes. Shared game-night text can include a nickname, chosen title, proposed time window or a memory. The app does not send these automatically. Data you send through Messages, AirDrop or another service is handled by that service and the people receiving it.

Retention and deletion

The app retains local content until you remove it. Settings → Erase everything on this device removes your shelf, imported lists, game nights, memories, cover photos, cached thumbnails and app settings. Your App Store purchase is not deleted. Files or images already shared outside the app remain with their recipients or destination services. Apple's device backup and the purchase providers' retention policies may apply to data those services hold.

Support and policy changes

If you email support, we receive your email address and the content you send and use it to respond to your request. Contact tangocards.support@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests. Material policy changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

決済はAppleが処理し、RevenueCatが購入と復元を確認します。RevenueCatは取引・レシート情報、匿名のアプリユーザー識別子、購入処理に必要な端末・アプリ情報を扱い、管理画面で購入に関する集計を行います。広告や、他社アプリ・サイトを横断するトラッキングは行いません。

共有されたリンクの情報を取得する際、AppleのLinkPresentationがリンク先へ接続し、IPアドレスなど通常の通信情報が相手のサイトに届く場合があります。サムネイルは端末内に保存し、共有カードやリストの書き出しには含めません。写真はAppleの写真選択画面で選んだものだけを利用します。

「設定 → この端末のデータをすべて削除」で端末内の記録、取り込んだリスト、写真、キャッシュ、設定を削除できます。購入済みの権利や、アプリ外へ送ったファイルは削除されません。Appleの端末バックアップや購入サービス側の保存方針が適用される場合があります。
