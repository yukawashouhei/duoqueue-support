Common questions

How do I save a game?

Add a title inside the app, or open the share sheet on a video or website and choose Duo Queue. Confirm the title and choose Now, Someday or With someone. The Share Extension becomes available for saving after you unlock the app.

How do I compare lists?

In Friends, send your list as a .duoqueue file through Messages, AirDrop or another sharing app. Your friend can open the file in Duo Queue. Shared games appear first. Games from another person's list are added to your shelf only when you choose Add to my shelf.

Is it a subscription?

No. An interactive fictional sample is available before purchase. One non-consumable purchase unlocks all features. The price is shown by the App Store before purchase.

How do I restore or use an invite code?

Choose Restore Purchases on the purchase screen or in Settings using the same Apple Account. Have an invite code? opens Apple's standard redemption sheet. Return to Duo Queue after redeeming; if access has not appeared, try Restore Purchases.

Does my library sync between devices?

Duo Queue has no account or app-operated cloud sync. Your library is stored locally on your device. Shared list files contain the fields needed to compare games; they are not a full backup of your notes, photos or purchase.

How do I delete my data?

Use Settings → Erase everything on this device. This removes your shelf, imported lists, cover photos and app settings. Your purchase remains associated with your Apple Account. Files or cards you already shared elsewhere must be removed from those destinations separately.
