Common questions

How do I save a game?

Add a title inside the app, or open the share sheet on a video or website and choose Duo Queue. Confirm the title and choose Now, Someday or With someone. Five saved games are free. Open Duo Queue once before saving your first game from another app.

How do we choose a game together?

Choose together on Tonight, shortlist up to three games, then pass the phone. Each person marks the games they would enjoy. The reveal only shows a mutual choice when both actually selected it. You can change the shortlist if there is no match. Choose a mutual game, share the proposal if you want, and save a memory afterward. Ending a session does not mark the game completed. A guest can participate without installing the app.

How do I compare lists?

In Friends, send your list as a .duoqueue file through Messages, AirDrop or another sharing app. Your friend can open the file in Duo Queue. Shared games appear first. Games from another person's list are added to your shelf only when you choose Add to my shelf.

Is it a subscription?

No. Five saved games and your first game night are free. A single purchase unlocks unlimited games and game nights. You can close the upgrade screen and keep using your saved content. The App Store supplies the current price.

How do I restore or use an invite code?

Choose Restore Purchases on the purchase screen or in Settings using the same Apple Account. Have an invite code? opens Apple's standard redemption sheet. Return to Duo Queue after redeeming; if access has not appeared, try Restore Purchases.

Does my library sync between devices?

Duo Queue has no account or app-operated cloud sync. Your library is stored locally on your device. Shared list files contain the fields needed to compare games; they are not a full backup of your notes, photos or purchase.

How do I delete my data?

Use Settings → Erase everything on this device. This removes your shelf, imported lists, game nights, memories, cover photos and app settings. Your purchase remains associated with your Apple Account. Files or cards you already shared elsewhere must be removed from those destinations separately.

ゲームを名前で追加するか、動画やWebサイトの共有シートからDuo Queueを選んで保存できます。別のアプリから初めて保存する前に、Duo Queueを一度開いてください。「友達」からリストをファイルで送り、受け取った.duoqueueファイルを開くと、二人のリストにあるゲームを比べられます。

ゲーム5本と最初のゲームナイトは無料です。候補を3本まで選んでスマホを渡すと、それぞれが遊びたいゲームを選べます。二人とも選んだゲームを今夜のプランにして、遊んだ後は思い出を残せます。友達はアプリを入れなくても同じスマホで参加できます。ゲームやゲームナイトを無制限に追加するには、買い切りの購入をご利用ください。サブスクリプションではありません。

「購入を復元」は購入画面と設定から使えます。招待コードはAppleの入力画面でご利用ください。記録は端末内に保存され、アプリ独自のクラウド同期はありません。「設定 → この端末のデータをすべて削除」で端末内のデータを削除できます。購入済みの権利はApple Accountに残ります。
