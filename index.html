Common questions

How do I save a game?

Open Discover, search a title, open a game and choose Add to my picks. The current catalog scope appears below search. When console search is not connected, FreeToGame covers free-to-play PC and browser games. You can also add any title yourself, use the iPhone share sheet, or take a picture of the box on the Scan tab. Five saved games and one scan are free.

How does Scan work, and what does it send?

Point the camera at a game box or a screen and press the shutter. The photo is shown exactly as taken and sent once, reduced to about 640 pixels, to our catalog service on Google Cloud, which asks OpenAI's API to name the game in the picture; the matched game's case then stands up in front of your photo (drag to turn it, double-tap to flip). Our service does not store the photo and does not link it to you; under OpenAI's API terms the picture is not used to train their models. If nothing matches, you can keep the photo as the game's cover on your device. The first scan is free; from the second, the app asks for Queue Pass before capturing anything. Tap the Scan tab again to take another picture.

How do we choose a game together?

Add games from My list to a room. Each person chooses Want to play, Pass for now, Playing or Finished. Use Send room update to share the .duoroom file. A friend opens it in Duo Queue, joins, chooses and sends it back. Open the returned file to merge changes. Old files cannot replace newer choices. Everyone’s picks shows the actual shared choices. Previous game nights remain accessible from Rooms.

How do I compare lists?

From Rooms, open Compare an older friend list and send your list as a .duoqueue file through Messages, AirDrop or another sharing app. Your friend can open the file in Duo Queue. Shared games appear first. Games from another person's list are added to your shelf only when you choose Add to my shelf.

Is it a subscription?

No. Five saved games, one room and one scan are free. A single purchase unlocks unlimited games, rooms, scans and game nights. You can close the upgrade screen and keep using your saved content. The App Store supplies the current price.

How do I restore or use an invite code?

Choose Restore Purchases on the purchase screen or in Settings using the same Apple Account. Have an invite code? opens Apple's standard redemption sheet. Return to Duo Queue after redeeming; if access has not appeared, try Restore Purchases.

Does my library sync between devices?

Duo Queue has no account or app-operated cloud sync. Your library is stored locally on your device. Shared list files contain the fields needed to compare games; they are not a full backup of your notes, photos or purchase.

How do I delete my data?

Use Settings → Erase everything on this device. This removes your shelf, imported lists, game nights, memories, cover photos and app settings. Your purchase remains associated with your Apple Account. Files or cards you already shared elsewhere must be removed from those destinations separately.

お問い合わせ先：tangocards.support@gmail.com。iPhoneの機種、iOSのバージョン、起きたことを添えてご連絡ください。説明に必要な場合を除き、ゲームのメモやリストファイルを送る必要はありません。

「探す」でゲームを検索し、作品を開いて「候補に入れる」で保存できます。検索対象は画面に明記します。家庭用ゲームの検索が未接続の場合は、PC・ブラウザの基本無料ゲームが対象です。対象外の作品も名前の手入力やiPhoneの共有シートから追加できます。「スキャン」タブでパッケージを撮ると、写真を当方の検索サービス（Google Cloud）へ1回だけ送り、OpenAIのAPIが写っているゲームを識別します。写真は保存されず、あなたと結び付けられることもありません。一致したゲームのケースが写真の手前に立ち上がり、ドラッグで回転、ダブルタップで裏返せます。一致しない場合は写真をそのままカバーとして端末内に保存できます。スキャンタブをもう一度タップすると撮り直せます。別のアプリから初めて保存する前に、Duo Queueを一度開いてください。「友達」からリストをファイルで送り、受け取った.duoqueueファイルを開くと、二人のリストにあるゲームを比べられます。

ゲーム5本・ルーム1つ・スキャン1回まで無料です。マイリストのゲームをルームへ追加し、それぞれが遊びたいゲームを選びます。.duoroomファイルを送り、友達が選択して返したファイルを開くと更新を反映できます。古いファイルで新しい選択が戻ることはありません。買い切りでゲーム・ルーム・スキャン・ゲームナイトを無制限に使えます。サブスクリプションではありません。

「購入を復元」は購入画面と設定から使えます。招待コードはAppleの入力画面でご利用ください。記録は端末内に保存され、アプリ独自のクラウド同期はありません。「設定 → この端末のデータをすべて削除」で端末内のデータを削除できます。購入済みの権利はApple Accountに残ります。
